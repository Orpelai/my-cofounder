# Execution Brief

Produce this compact artifact:

## Goal and gap

- Outcome, baseline, target, current value, deadline, and evidence source.
- Binding constraint or decision.

## Selection

- Primary playbook path and trigger fit.
- Supporting playbook path, only if required.
- Rejected close alternatives and reason.
- Evidence strength and important adaptation limits.

## Readiness

- Available required inputs.
- Missing inputs and smallest way to obtain them.
- Authority, privacy, budget, and safety boundaries.

## Execution

- Owner and collaborators.
- Three to seven ordered steps with decision gates.
- Immediate next action and expected completion time.
- Deliverable or output contract.

## Measurement

- Outcome, leading, input, and guardrail measures.
- Evidence threshold and review cadence.
- Continue, adapt, stop, or escalate rules.

Keep facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and recommendations visibly separate.
