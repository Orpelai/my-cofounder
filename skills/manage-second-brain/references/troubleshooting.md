# Troubleshooting

## Nothing found

Search aliases, language variants, dates, related entities, recent daily pages, and the inbox. Report “not found”; never invent.

## Conflicting records

Preserve both, compare dates and source quality, and state a current conclusion only when evidence supports it.

## Duplicate pages

Keep the page with stronger provenance, merge only unique supported information, preserve useful history, and repair links.

## Page too large

Split by concept, entity, decision, or reusable learning; leave a concise routing page.

## Sensitive information captured

Stop propagation, follow `SECURITY.md`, quarantine or remove the sensitive material only with required approval, rotate exposed credentials when applicable, inspect derived pages and history, and retain only a safe incident record.

## Binary found in the second brain

Stop ingestion. Move or remove it only with required approval, place the payload under the repository-level `assets/` convention with a complete sibling sidecar and stable backup locator, and replace the raw payload with a textual source record linking all three.
