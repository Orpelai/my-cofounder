# Maintenance and Lint

## After a material ingest

Check touched links, provenance, claim labels, duplicates, index entries, sensitivity, freshness, and contradictions.

## Periodically

Find stale claims, unsupported facts, broken links, orphan pages, synonymous duplicates, missing asset metadata, unprocessed sources, and overexposed sensitive material. Prioritize defects most likely to distort a current decision.

## Repair rules

- Preserve raw evidence.
- Correct compiled pages with cited evidence.
- Record contradictions instead of averaging them away.
- Supersede historical decisions; do not silently rewrite them.
- Append corrections to observation history rather than replacing old measurements.
- Record unresolved defects in `wiki/gaps/` and log material repairs.
