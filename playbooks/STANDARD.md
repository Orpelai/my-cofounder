# Playbook Quality Standard

Every playbook must include:

1. precise trigger and “do not use when”;
2. required evidence and minimum inputs;
3. owner, decider, contributors, and escalation;
4. time-boxed procedure and decision gates;
5. concrete output contract;
6. outcome, leading, input, and guardrail metrics;
7. failure modes and ethical/financial/legal/reputational guardrails;
8. direct primary or authoritative sources;
9. adaptation notes and evidence-strength label;
10. review date and learning history.

Never admit a framework merely because it is famous. Never strip away caveats or represent vendor methodology as causal proof.
